Aston Business Intelligence — logo & colour

The ABi monogram, rebuilt as vector in white, gold and charcoal. This page is the single reference for anyone producing material for Aston: which file to use, which colours, and what not to do. All files live in the repo under design/assets/.

01

Primary lockup

lockup-solid · on white
lockup-solid-reversed · on ink

Default for everything: website header, documents, email signatures, print. Flat gold — no gradient — so it reproduces correctly anywhere.

02

Metallic (screen only)

lockup-metallic
badge · 512 px

For hero sections, slide title pages, video idents and the app icon / avatar. Never for print or anything below 48 px tall — the gradient turns to mud.

03

Mark alone & stacked

mark-solid · mark-metallic · mark-mono-ink
stacked-solid

Use the mark alone only when the name appears elsewhere on the same surface. Stacked lockup for square spaces: social posts, badges, event banners. Mono versions for single-colour print, embroidery, engraving.

04

Colour

Ink#17161F

Paper#FFFFFF

Gold#E9B324

Gold on dark#FFC93C

Gold text on white#C98A06

Gold deep#B47A06

Gold highlight#FFE58A

Warm grey#F6F5F1

Rule: words in ink, gold in the symbol. Gold is never used for body text on white; for headings or labels in gold on white, use #C98A06. Buttons: gold fill, ink text.

05

Clear space & minimum size

  • Keep a margin equal to the height of the A on all sides.
  • Lockup: minimum 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.
  • Mark alone: minimum 24 px; use the badge below 24 px (favicons).

06

Don’t

  • Don’t recolour the mark — no red, no blue, no black-on-gold.
  • Don’t use the metallic version in print or at small sizes.
  • Don’t put the gold lockup on a photo or coloured background; use the reversed or mono-white version on a dark overlay.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, add shadows or outlines, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
  • Don’t use the old red ABi raster anywhere.
