"Reconciled to the ledger" — five treatments

Section 04 of the Data & BI page. The point is trust: the dashboard and the accounts agree, and you can see it. Each option proves that differently. (One option also shows what happens when a number doesn't tie back — that honesty is half the pitch.)

A

The live check

The existing table, brought to life: a gold scan line sweeps down, each row ticks green as it passes — and one row is £3,000 out, so it's flagged gold with the reason, not hidden. Footer updates to "3 of 4 tie back · 1 flagged". Loops.
