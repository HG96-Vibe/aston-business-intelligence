Helix — mixes of D, E and B

Four ways to keep what's premium about D (the gold, the single elegant object) while giving the copy a charcoal home (E) and letting the monogram be lit rather than placed (B). Same image in all four; only the grading, the gradient and the monogram change.

F

Charcoal → gold, monogram at the seam

The left half is charcoal, the right half is D's gold almost untouched, and the gradient between them is where the traced ABi sits — half in shadow, half in light. You get D's premium gold and E's legible copy in one frame; the monogram marks the transition.
