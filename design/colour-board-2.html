Home hero — gradient directions

Gradients, with indigo as the anchor in most. Gold stays as the accent and button colour everywhere — on indigo it reads even brighter than on charcoal. Two of these (O and P) keep the rest of the site light and only colour the hero; the others go dark site-wide.

I

Indigo → violet

A clean diagonal from deep indigo to violet. Saturated, modern, unmistakably "tech". Gold pops hard against it. The most literal answer to "indigo gradient".

J

Deep space

Near-black with an indigo nebula glowing top-right behind the monogram and a violet bloom bottom-left. Dark and quiet with colour where it matters. Premium rather than loud.

K

Aurora

Indigo, a touch of teal and a hint of gold as soft overlapping lights that drift very slowly (14s). Alive without being a video. The most "striking" of the set on a large screen.

L

Sapphire → emerald

Deep blue sliding into deep green. Cooler and more corporate than indigo-violet; reads as finance-meets-sustainability. Gold sits comfortably on both ends.

M

Dusk

Indigo on the left warming through amber into gold on the right, so the brand colour is the gradient. Dramatic; the copy sits on the cool side, the monogram on the warm side. Needs care with contrast on the gold end.

N

Plum → indigo

Warmer than I: starts in deep plum and cools into indigo-blue. Rich and slightly regal. Less common than straight indigo, so more ownable.

O

Light mesh

Stay light, but replace flat paper with a soft indigo wash top-right and a gold wash bottom — colour as atmosphere behind a near-white ground. Premium without going dark; everything else on the site stays as-is.

P

Indigo hero, light site

Only the hero goes indigo; the credentials strip and everything below return to white paper. A strong opening frame without committing the whole site to dark. Easiest to adopt — one section changes.
