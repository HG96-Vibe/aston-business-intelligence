"Five views" — five treatments

Section 03 of the Data & BI page: the five dashboards. All five options use real mini-dashboards (illustrative numbers, labelled). They differ in how the owner's question is tied to the screen.

A

Workstation

Five questions down the left; the dashboard on the right swaps to answer whichever is selected. Autoplays through all five (6s each) with a gold progress rail; click any question to jump. One big, legible screen at a time.

B

Question cards

Five paper cards, each carrying one question in italic serif. Hover or tap flips a card to reveal the dashboard that answers it. Playful, compact; all five visible at once.

A month in the business

The fan

Question → answer
