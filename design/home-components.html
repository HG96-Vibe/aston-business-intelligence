Objections FAQ

Answers the five things people think but don't ask on the call.

Before you ask

The questions people usually have.

We're too small for a consultancy, aren't we?
If you have more than one spreadsheet that matters and more than one person who touches it, you're not. Our first engagement costs less than a month of a junior analyst's salary and tells you whether anything bigger is worth doing.
Will you try to sell us a platform?
No. We build on what you already pay for — Xero, Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, HubSpot. If something new is needed we'll say so in writing with the price, and you can buy it from anyone.
What if we don't like the findings?
You keep the report. It's written so any other consultant or an internal hire could act on it. The fee is credited against work with us within 12 months, but there's no obligation.
How much of our team's time will this take?
About four hours across the two weeks — a kick-off, a few 30-minute conversations, and a read-through at the end. We work around month-end.
Is our data safe with you?
Read-only access, UK-based processing, an NDA before we see anything if you want one, and nothing leaves your systems without being written into the plan first.
