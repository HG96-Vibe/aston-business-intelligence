"What we do" — four treatments

Section 03 of the home page: the four services. Same four photographs. The question is how much room each service gets and how the reader moves between them.

B

Accordion strip

Four tall photo panels side by side; hover (or tap) expands one to show its outcomes and link, the others narrow to a title. Full-height photography, one service in focus at a time. The most striking use of the images.

C

Editorial rows

Each service gets a full-width row: a ghosted number, the name and one line, the three outcomes, and the photo — alternating sides. Generous and unhurried; reads like a magazine spread. Longest of the four.

D

Tabs with one large image

Service names down the left (same pattern as the five views on the Data & BI page); the selected one shows its photo large on the right with outcomes beneath. Compact, consistent with the rest of the site, and the photo gets real size.

E

Stacked cards on scroll

Four large cards, each half photo, half text. As you scroll, each one pins and the next slides up over it, so they stack like a deck. Scroll this frame to see it. Tactile and modern; needs the section to be tall, which it earns.
