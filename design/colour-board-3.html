Home hero — light directions

Staying light, but off the warm paper. Tinted greys and soft greens as the ground, charcoal or forest for type and buttons, gold kept as the accent (dialled to a deeper bronze where the ground is cool, so it doesn't go mustard). Night mode stays as it is in every case.

Q

Sage

A grey-green ground — think Farrow & Ball rather than mint. Calm, expensive, very British. Charcoal type with a green undertone; the dot grid in deep sage. Quietly different from everyone.

R

Eucalyptus

Fresher and cooler than sage: a pale mint-grey ground with a deep eucalyptus green for buttons and ink. Reads as clean and modern — health-tech, fintech. The green is the brand here; gold steps back to a supporting role.

S

Graphite

Cool light grey with a hint of blue, near-black type, gold unchanged. The most "serious software" of the light options — precise and technical. Separates from Augustova's warmth without adding a colour.

T

Stone

A deeper greige — darker than paper, warmer than grey, like unpolished limestone. Gold goes bronze. Feels architectural and solid; the hero reads as a surface rather than a page.

U

Charcoal hero, light site

Not black: a mid charcoal with a subtle gradient, gold at full brightness, white paper resuming from the credentials strip down. A strong first frame that still leaves the rest of the site light.

V

Sage wash

Near-white ground with a soft sage cloud behind the monogram and a faint gold wash bottom-left. Lighter touch than Q — the green is atmosphere, not a surface. Easy to live with.

W

Pale slate

Light blue-grey, like morning sky over the City. Cool, trustworthy, finance-adjacent without going navy. Gold warms it up nicely.

X

Warm grey + forest

Keep a warm-grey ground close to today's, but swap the charcoal buttons and italic for a deep forest green, with bronze-gold as the second accent. The racing-green idea, translated into a light theme.
